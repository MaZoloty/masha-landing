# Product Brief

## Project

Maria Zolotukhina's personal site and funnel ecosystem for websites, funnels, AI automation, analytics, and service-business client paths.

## Audience

Owners and managers of beauty, wellness, local service, and expert businesses. They are not looking for a toy quiz or a decorative landing page. They need clarity, trust, and a sense that the page understands money, operations, bookings, admins, client return, and analytics.

## Business Role

The site should turn attention into a concrete next step:

- free diagnostic or useful tool;
- paid low-ticket product such as a solution map;
- paid review or consultation;
- implementation or larger project.

## Product Feeling

The interface should feel like:

- a polished consulting dashboard;
- a decision-support tool for a business owner;
- a calm expert system;
- a commercial page that sells through usefulness, not pressure.

It should not feel like:

- a cheap quiz;
- an info-product landing page;
- a generic startup SaaS template;
- a rough MVP;
- a decorative beauty page with weak business logic.

## Key UX Principles

- Start with the user's business problem, not with Maria's internal funnel logic.
- Show the next useful step, not just the next paid step.
- Avoid zero-value placeholders on first screens, such as `0 ₽` or `0 баллов`, unless the user has already interacted.
- Result blocks should explain what the numbers mean and what decision follows.
- Paid offers should be framed as reducing uncertainty or helping choose the correct level of solution.
- Forms should feel like a continuation of the diagnostic, not a sudden lead-capture wall.

## Current Important Funnel

`/diagnostika/` is a standalone diagnostic page:

1. Hero with preliminary report framing.
2. Step 1: base business numbers.
3. Step 2: 12 audit points.
4. Dashboard-style result.
5. Top-3 priority leaks.
6. Solution map offer.
7. Soft consultation/review block.

Standalone funnel pages should not automatically inherit the main site header.
